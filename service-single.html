STEP BY STEP

How Meta Ads Work

Our end-to-end process from strategy to scaling.

01

Goal & Strategy

We understand your business, product, and target customers. Based on your goals, we choose the right Meta Ads strategy — lead generation, traffic, engagement, messages or conversions.

02

Audience Research

We research your ideal customers and build relevant audiences using location, age, interests, behaviors, and demographics for precise targeting.

03

Ad Creative & Copy

We create scroll-stopping ad creatives, videos, headlines, and CTAs designed to capture attention and drive action from your target audience.

04

Campaign Setup

We set up your Meta campaign structure, objectives, ad sets, placements, budgets, schedules, and conversion tracking for a flawless launch.

05

Launch & Testing

Once ready, we launch campaigns and test different audiences, creatives, messages, and offers to identify the best-performing combinations.

06

Monitor & Optimize

We continuously monitor reach, clicks, CTR, CPC, leads, and conversions. Underperforming ads are adjusted, paused, or replaced.

07

Retargeting

We reconnect with people who previously interacted with your ads, website, or social pages — turning interested visitors into leads and customers.

08

Results & Growth

We analyze final results and use performance insights to improve future campaigns, scale winning ads, and build a consistent acquisition system.
